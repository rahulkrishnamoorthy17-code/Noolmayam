Privacy policy

How this concept storefront handles information in the browser.

Updated 10 November 2026 · Frontend demonstration

1. Purpose

NOOLMAYAM is an illustrative frontend project. This page explains what the demo saves and what it does not do.

2. Contact forms

The enquiry form asks for a name, email address, phone number, enquiry topic and message. The information is saved locally in your browser storage on this device. This frontend does not email enquiries to a store.

3. Newsletter interest

Submitting a newsletter address saves the entered email in browser storage for the demo. It does not subscribe you to a mailing list or send marketing messages.

4. Your choices

You can clear the stored information by clearing this site’s browser storage or website data. Avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information to a demo storefront.

5. Third-party resources

The site may load fonts from Google Fonts and an illustrative OpenStreetMap embed. When those resources load, their providers may receive technical request information under their own policies.

6. Updates and contact

This sample privacy notice was prepared on 10 November 2026. Before a real store launch, this notice must be reviewed and updated with the operator’s verified contact details and actual data practices.
